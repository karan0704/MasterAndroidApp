# Home Scope

## Current Goal

Provide a stable home screen that proves:
- modular structure
- clean feature ownership
- future scalability

## In Scope

- current date and time display
- center content area
- acting as a launch surface for future modules

## Out Of Scope For This Feature Right Now

- large business workflows
- sync
- authentication
- network-heavy logic
- advanced multi-module orchestration

## Scope Notes

The home feature should remain light.
Its main purpose is foundation and composition, not heavy business logic.
